## Overview
ZonePark is a parking-information concept intended to help drivers understand parking-area type, eligibility, restrictions, operating hours, price and next action before leaving the car. The first project context is Hungary.

## Problem
Parking information may be spread across street signs, municipal websites, parking operators and map services. A driver in an unfamiliar area may struggle to determine whether parking is permitted and what conditions apply.

## Target users
- Drivers visiting an unfamiliar city or district
- Visitors who may not understand local parking terminology
- Residents parking outside their familiar area

## Research basis
Two initial interviews were conducted. Both participants described difficulty finding payment information; one also described language-related difficulty understanding rules and prices. Three different participants later reviewed the prototype. Their feedback was qualitative and generally positive, with concerns around complexity and trust in official data. The samples are small and findings should not be generalized.

## Design direction
Present the parking-area status and eligibility before payment. Explain restrictions and operating times. Only show payment as a next step when the area is public paid parking. If information is missing or uncertain, do not imply parking is allowed.
