# Research summary
## Discovery interviews
Two drivers were interviewed in the initial discovery stage. Both described difficulty finding parking payment information. One also reported difficulty understanding rules and prices because of a language barrier and relied on friends for help. The other had previously received a parking ticket and asked other people where to pay.

## Prototype feedback
Three separate participants reviewed the prototype. Feedback was generally positive. Participants described relying on search, friends, existing parking apps or AI to understand parking conditions. One specifically asked for more official/government information and broader country coverage.

## Interpretation
The findings suggest that clear payment guidance, understandable rules and trustworthy source information deserve attention. This is a small qualitative sample, not evidence of how common these problems are among all drivers.

## Evidence limits
Interview notes and tester feedback are paraphrased. No verbatim quotations were captured. Formal usability metrics such as task success rate, completion time, help requests and confidence ratings were not recorded.
