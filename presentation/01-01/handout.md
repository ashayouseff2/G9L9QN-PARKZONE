## Project overview
Park Zone is a parking-information concept for drivers in unfamiliar areas. It focuses on helping a driver understand the area type, eligibility, operating hours, restrictions, price and next action before leaving the car. The initial project context is Hungary.

## Problem and users
Drivers may need to combine signs, city websites, parking-provider information, maps or help from other people to understand parking rules. The project focuses on visitors and drivers parking in unfamiliar districts.

## Research
Two initial driver interviews informed the early problem exploration. Both participants described difficulty finding payment information; one also described language-related difficulty understanding rules and prices. These are preliminary qualitative findings from a small sample.

Three separate participants later reviewed the clickable prototype. Feedback was generally positive, while participants also raised complexity and trust/data-source concerns. Feedback was recorded as qualitative summaries; formal completion times, per-person success rates and confidence scores were not collected.

## Design response
1. Select or confirm a location.
2. View the area type and eligibility.
3. Read restrictions, hours and conditions.
4. Continue to payment details only when the area is public paid parking.
5. If the area or rules cannot be verified, show an explicit uncertainty state rather than implying permission.

## Prototype and limitations
The prototype demonstrates location entry, an example zone result, a payment-demo screen, place information, a demo confirmation and an unavailable-location state. It does not use live municipal data, geocoding or a real payment service. Prices and parking hours shown in the demo are illustrative. Users must follow official signs and authority information.

## Next steps
Validate the flow with more drivers, measure task completion and time, test comprehension of eligibility and exceptions, and investigate access to authoritative parking data for each city.
