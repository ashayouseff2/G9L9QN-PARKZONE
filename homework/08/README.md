# Homework 08 — Usability testing and mini UI guideline
## Test plan
Three anonymised prototype testers (P1, P2, P3). Scenario: parked in an unfamiliar street; determine conditions before leaving the car. Success criterion: find relevant parking information and choose an appropriate next action without help. Intended measures: task completion, time, mistakes/hesitation, help and confidence (1–5).
## Consent briefing
“I’m testing the Park Zone prototype, not you. This should take about 5 minutes. There are no right or wrong answers. Please think aloud. The prototype uses example information only; payment is not real. I will take anonymous notes, not your name. You can stop at any time. Is it okay to begin?”
## Participant observations
**P1 — Sarah, frequent driver:** travels to different areas; has received tickets where restrictions were unclear; tried internet search and AI. Feedback summary: she and Lena considered the app good.
**P2 — Lena, moderate driver:** does not drive regularly; calls a Hungarian friend when maps do not explain whether parking is paid; Google Maps did not resolve the question. Feedback summary: she and Sarah considered it good.
**P3 — Alexa, frequent driver:** uses Parker and AI for parking rules; asked for more official government information across countries; found the prototype easy to use and understand.
## Findings
Trust/data coverage: P3 requested official cross-country data; P1 described uncertainty around restrictions. Some steps may feel complicated despite the overall smooth flow. Overall feedback was positive.
## Fixes
Make status/next action easier to scan; show source and verification status; keep buttons consistent and labels short.
## Measurement limitations
No completion time, independent task success, help count, confidence score or verbatim quotes were recorded. Qualitative feedback only.
## Mini UI guideline
Colours: navy #142B4A headings; blue #2563EB primary actions; green #16A34A success; background #F1F5F9; red #DC2626 errors. Typography: heading 24px bold, body 16px, caption 12px. Spacing: 8px related items, 16px sections, 24px major groups. Visible labels, focus states, clear errors, colour-independent status, contrast targets 4.5:1 normal and 3:1 large text. Formal contrast audit not completed.
