# Homework 07 — Clickable prototype
Source: Figma page “Homework 7 — Clickable prototype”.
## Six screens
1. Start/choose location — current location or address; no account needed.
2. Zone result — example Zone 2 · Central, paid status, sample hours/price, payment and place information.
3. Payment details — session, duration, example total and payment method.
4. Place information — restrictions and official-source guidance.
5. Payment success — prototype confirmation only, not a real receipt.
6. Location unavailable — unclear zone; do not assume parking is allowed.
## Interactions
“Pay for parking” opens Payment details. “Place information” opens rules and official source. Unknown data leads to exception state.
## Data safety
Zone 2, €1.20/hour and hours are examples only. No real money is charged. Users must check signs and official sources. Prototype is not a live service.
