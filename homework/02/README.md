# User research
This week I conducted two initial interviews with drivers in Hungary. Both participants described difficulty finding parking payment information. One participant also reported difficulty understanding rules and prices because of a language barrier.

The interview notes are anonymised and paraphrased. These are preliminary qualitative findings from two participants.
