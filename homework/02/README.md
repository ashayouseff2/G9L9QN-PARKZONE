# Homework 02 — User research
Two short interviews with drivers in Hungary. Notes are paraphrased, not verbatim; participants are anonymised.
## Participant 1
Last drove in Hungary last semester. Had difficulty finding the parking payment booth, asked other people where to pay, had previously received a parking ticket, and found parking information displayed on the payment booth.
## Participant 2
Last drove in Hungary one month ago. Described finding parking as very difficult, could not find clear signs explaining where to pay or how much, and had difficulty understanding rules due to a language barrier. Friends helped.
## Affinity themes
1. Finding payment: both participants described difficulty locating payment information.
2. Understanding rules/prices: Participant 2 reported unclear amounts and difficulty understanding rules.
3. Help from others: Participant 1 asked people where to pay; Participant 2 had friends helping.
4. Ticket risk: Participant 1 had received a ticket previously.
## Insights
**Payment location:** Both reported difficulty finding where to pay. Explore clear payment locations and methods.
**Rules and language:** One participant reported difficulty understanding rules and prices. Explore plain-language explanations and language support.
## Limits and next steps
Preliminary findings from two interviews only. No verbatim quotes were recorded. Clarify what “finding parking” meant, then validate the persona and a specific parking journey.
