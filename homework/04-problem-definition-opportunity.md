# Homework 04 — Problem definition and opportunity search
## Motivation
The project is motivated by the project owner's experience of receiving a parking ticket after parking in an area that seemed permissible. Online information was unclear and clarification required visiting the parking service office. This is personal motivation, not interview evidence.
## Research basis
Two anonymised interviews: both participants had difficulty finding payment information; one also had difficulty understanding rules/prices due to a language barrier. Findings support investigating information clarity, not the prevalence of permit-only confusion.
## Project brief
- **Problem:** Drivers in unfamiliar areas may not know whether parking is public, paid, permit-only or otherwise restricted.
- **Target:** Driver parking in an unfamiliar Hungarian city/district, especially a visitor.
- **Primary task:** Determine whether this specific area allows this driver to park and what conditions apply.
- **Goal:** Support informed decisions and reduce avoidable confusion.
- **Constraints:** Rules vary by city; information must be accurate, current, understandable and traceable.
- **Success criterion:** In a usability test, driver correctly identifies whether they may park and explains conditions.
- **Exclusions:** No space booking, enforcement/appeals, guaranteed ticket prevention, or claim ZonePark grants permits.
## Stakeholders
Drivers/visitors; residents/permit holders; municipalities/parking authorities; parking operators; ZonePark team. Authority/operator data access is not confirmed.
## User story
As a driver parking in an unfamiliar area, I want to check whether I am allowed to park and what conditions apply, so I can decide before leaving my car.
## How might we?
Help drivers understand area type, determine eligibility, understand restrictions, and know what to do when special conditions apply.
## Solution ideas
Area-type classification; eligibility checklist; zone detail page; map boundary labels; plain-language rules; before-you-park checklist; official-source/update indicator; alternative action guidance; payment eligibility guide.
## Initial impact × feasibility
High/high: zone detail, plain-language rules. High/lower: eligibility checklist, area classification. Lower/high: checklist, source indicator. Lower/lower: precise boundary mapping, alternative guidance, payment eligibility. Initial estimate, not measured evidence.
## Direction
Show area type, who may park, restrictions/conditions and official source. Assumptions to test: permit-only confusion prevalence, official data integration feasibility, and user trust.
