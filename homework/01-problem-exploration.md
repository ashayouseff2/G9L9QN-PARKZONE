# Homework 01 — Problem exploration
## Starting point
Parking information in Hungary.
## Problem
Drivers in unfamiliar cities need to understand where they can park, what it costs and which restrictions apply. Information can be scattered across city websites and parking providers.
## Affected users
People travelling between Hungarian cities; visitors and tourists; residents parking in unfamiliar districts.
## User need
Find accurate parking rules and expected costs before leaving the car.
## Product challenge
Present city-specific information clearly and keep it up to date.
## Problem statement
People driving in unfamiliar Hungarian cities need one clear way to understand parking zones, prices and restrictions because information is spread across multiple sources.
## Questions to validate
Where do drivers look for parking information? Which details are hardest to understand? Which official data sources are available? Would users trust a combined service?
